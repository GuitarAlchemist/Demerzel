## Tasks

1. A designer proposes one bubble chart for all five values. State two problems with that display and write the two comparison questions, each with its unit.
2. Place the QA rates on one percentage position scale. Calculate their difference in percentage points. If the display shows only 70–100%, what must its label or caption disclose? What improvement claim remains unverified?
3. Draw or specify three zero-origin count bars. If guitar is 100 units long, calculate bass and ukulele lengths to two decimals. Explain how you would let a reader compare bass with ukulele without silently truncating a bar.
4. Suppose circles represent guitar and bass counts. Calculate the circle radius ratio needed for areas proportional to counts. Explain the distortion caused by using the count ratio directly as a radius ratio.
5. Replace this caption with two sentences that preserve the dates, units, denominators, and evidence limits: “GA quality grew from 75% to 98.08%; guitar is 38 times better than bass.”
