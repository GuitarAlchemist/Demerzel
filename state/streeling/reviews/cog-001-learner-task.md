# COG-001 learner transfer task

Give this sheet **without** the COG-001 answer blocks or review brief. The scenario is invented for teaching; none of these counts describes GA or Learn.

A team plans **15** Streeling lessons. Six lessons have passed a **local preview**; nine have not been previewed. Two public URLs were checked at a recorded time: one returned the expected page, one returned 404. The other thirteen public URLs were not checked. No human-review receipts were supplied. A colleague's first estimate is “90% ready.” The team has already spent six months on the workflow. For the next quarter it forecasts four hours of benefit and seven hours of maintenance per week.

Answer in your own words:

1. Separate observed preview, human-review, and public-publication evidence. What would you request before claiming all fifteen lessons are reviewed and public?
2. Compute checked passing local-preview coverage using the **planned** set as denominator. Why is the count of six visible successes alone misleading?
3. State exactly what the two public checks establish and what remains unknown. Does either 6/6 or 1/2 measure publication of all fifteen lessons?
4. Propose one proportionate next action for the 404 and one measurement that could change a longer-term release policy.
5. Compute the forecast weekly net hours and decide whether the six elapsed months should affect the next-quarter decision. Name one assumption you would validate.

State one observation that would falsify your release conclusion. Do not infer any actual learner effect from this paper exercise. The reviewer will retain your unedited response and use the separate five-criterion scorecard; no learner result exists yet.
