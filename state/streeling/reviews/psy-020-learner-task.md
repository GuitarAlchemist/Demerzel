# PSY-020 learner task — no answer key

You have read **When the Measure Becomes the Target: Citations as a Proxy**. Complete this transfer task without opening the course answer blocks. The wiki below is hypothetical: it is not Streeling data and not a forecast.

A team wiki has five handbooks, each with an address: `handbooks/north`, `handbooks/east`, `handbooks/south`, `handbooks/west` and `handbooks/centre`. A handbook's *link count* is the number of wiki pages that contain its address as a literal string, outside the folder `trash`; the handbook's own page is not counted. Today the counts are north 30, east 12, south 11, west 4 and centre 0.

1. Apply the rule to these four pages and say which of them count for east, with the reason for each. Which page actually uses the east handbook, and is it counted?
   - `teams/ops/runbook.md`: "Follow the east handbook, section 3, before each release."
   - `index/all-handbooks.md`: "handbooks/north handbooks/east handbooks/south handbooks/west handbooks/centre"
   - `trash/old-index.md`: "handbooks/north handbooks/east"
   - `handbooks/east` (the east handbook's own page): "Permanent address: handbooks/east"
2. Start again from today's counts. (a) A new index page lists all five addresses. Give the new counts, the new sum and its relative change, the relative change of the largest count and of the smallest non-zero count, and the number of order relations (which of two handbooks has more links) that change among the 10 pairs. (b) Instead, two new pages each give only the south address. Which order relation changes? (c) The wiki puts every handbook with 0 links up for removal. Which handbooks are put up before and after the index page of (a)? (d) The wiki freezes page creation when `new pages in the month / max(new links in the month, 1)` is above 3. In a month with 8 new pages and no new link, compute the ratio; then add the index page of (a) to that month and compute it again.
3. Each team has one unit of effort. Work yields `a` points of real value, which show one for one in the link count; inflation yields `g` links and no value. Take `a` and `g` from 1 to 4 (16 equally likely kinds of team, no measurement error) and a reward `(1 - w) x value + w x links`; a team inflates when that pays more than working. For `w = 1/2` and `w = 1`, count the teams that inflate, give the mean link count and the mean real value, and count the teams with 4 or more links and how many of them have a real value of 0. With a few lines of code, add the correlation between links and value.
4. East has 12 links. A forecast of east's next count will be published on a wiki page that contains east's address. Readers react: for each point the forecast stands above 12, they add 1/4 of a link. (a) Forecast 12: what is the outcome, and the error? (b) Which forecast equals its own outcome? (c) A forecaster who republishes the last outcome forecasts 13 in the second round: what is the outcome, and the error? (d) How could the forecast be published so that it does not move the count, and by what criterion?
5. A team lead claims that the link count measures how much each handbook is used, and wants to reward the teams whose handbooks gain links. Write a test, fixed before any new data arrive, that could show the count does not measure use: what you would sample and classify, by which written criterion, which result would falsify the claim, and one guard the reward rule needs. Say what your test cannot establish.

Return your calculations, your plan and your uncertainty. Write "not assessed" where evidence is absent. The reviewer scores the unedited response separately; this task contains no answers.
