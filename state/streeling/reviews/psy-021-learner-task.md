# PSY-021 learner task — no answer key

You have read **Conservation Claims You Can Check**. Complete this transfer task without opening the course answer blocks. The depot below is hypothetical: it is not Streeling data.

A parcel depot has three places: intake, sorting and the shelf. Every movement is scanned, and the scan log says:

- scans 1 to 10 registered parcels p01 to p10 at intake, one parcel per scan;
- scan 11 moved p01, p02, p03, p04, p05, p06, p07 and p10 from intake to sorting;
- scan 12 returned p09 from intake to its sender, so it left the depot;
- scan 13 moved p01, p02, p03, p04, p05, p06 and p10 from sorting to the shelf.

This morning a clerk walked through the depot without looking at the log and wrote down what each place holds. The count sheet says: intake holds p08; sorting holds p03 and p07; the shelf holds p01, p02, p03, p04, p05 and p06.

1. From the log alone, give for each place the number of parcels scanned in, the number scanned out, and the number the log expects to find there now.
2. How many parcels were registered, and how many entries does the count sheet hold? Check the statement "parcels registered = entries on the sheet", then the statement "parcels registered = entries on the sheet + parcels that left on record". Say for each whether it holds.
3. Compare, place by place, the number the log expects with the number of entries for that place. Which places are out of balance, and by how much? Explain how one of the statements of question 2 can hold while places are out of balance.
4. Compare name by name. Which parcel does the log expect that the sheet does not list, which parcel is listed where the log does not put it, and which parcel is listed in two places? Say what the counts of questions 2 and 3 could tell you and what they could not.
5. The depot manager says: "Throughput at this depot has momentum, and momentum is conserved." Give this statement one of the lesson's four labels and the criterion you used. Then rewrite it as a claim that could fail: name the quantity, the record that would have to be kept apart from it, and one outcome that would refute your version. Say what your version still cannot establish.

Return your calculations, your labels, your rewritten claim and your uncertainty. Write "not assessed" where evidence is absent. The reviewer scores the unedited response separately; this task contains no answers.
