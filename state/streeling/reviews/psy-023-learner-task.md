# PSY-023 learner task — no answer key

You have read **Monitoring a Plan: Tolerance Bands and Alarm Rules**. Complete this transfer task without opening the course answer blocks. The team below is hypothetical: it is not Streeling data and not a forecast. Every answer can be found by hand, by counting, or with a few lines of code; no simulation is needed.

A two-person team plans to finish 1 course a week. In a normal week it finishes 0, 1 or 2 courses, each with probability 1/3, independently of every other week. The *shortfall* after t weeks is the planned total, t courses, minus the courses actually finished.

1. **One look.** Give the mean and the variance of one week's output, and the standard deviation of the shortfall after 4 weeks. Then count, among the 3^4 = 81 equally likely histories of 4 weeks, those whose shortfall at the end of week 4 is at least 2 courses, and give the probability of that single look.
2. **A weekly limit.** The team raises an alarm at the end of any week in which it finishes nothing. Give the alarm chance of one normal week and the average run length. What is the probability of at least one alarm within 4 normal weeks, and within 12? After a slowdown, each week gives 0 or 1 course with probability 1/2 each: what is the mean number of slow weeks until this rule fires?
3. **A tolerance checked every week.** The team instead raises an alarm at the end of the first week at which the shortfall is at least 2, and checks every week for 4 normal weeks. Count the histories, among the 81, in which this rule fires at least once, and give the probability. Give also the probability of the single look at the end of each of weeks 1 to 4, and their sum. Explain why the probability of at least one alarm lies between the largest single look and that sum, and why it is not equal to either.
4. **A record.** Over 12 weeks the team finished 2, 1, 2, 2, 0, 1, 0, 0, 1, 0, 1, 0 courses; the slowdown began in week 5. Compute, week by week, the cumulative shortfall and Page's cumulative sum with reference value 1/4 and decision interval 2, written as C(t) = max(0, C(t − 1) + 1 − output(t) − 1/4). In which week does the tolerance of 2 fire, and in which week does the cumulative sum fire? How many slow weeks has each seen by then, and why does the tolerance fire later?
5. **A plan you could defend.** The team will monitor its next 52 weeks. Write a monitoring rule fixed before any of those weeks are seen: the rule and its threshold, the false-alarm probability you calibrated it to over the 52 weeks and on which model of normal weeks, the slowdown you want to detect and the delay you accept, and what the team does after an alarm. Name one observation of the coming weeks that would show the model behind your threshold to be wrong. Say what your plan cannot establish.

Return your counts, your calculations, your plan and your uncertainty. Write "not assessed" where evidence is absent. The reviewer scores the unedited response separately; this task contains no answers.
